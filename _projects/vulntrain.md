## Training models for vulnerability intelligence

VulnTrain provides a reproducible workflow for preparing vulnerability data and training machine-learning models that can support vulnerability analysis. It brings the steps required to build and publish these resources together in an open-source toolkit.

The software is developed by **CIRCL (Computer Incident Response Center Luxembourg)** as part of its contribution to AIPITCH and is maintained openly on GitHub.

## Models and datasets

The resources generated with VulnTrain are published through CIRCL's Hugging Face organisation:

- Explore the [machine-learning models generated by VulnTrain](https://huggingface.co/CIRCL/models).
- Browse the [associated datasets](https://huggingface.co/CIRCL/datasets) used to support training and research.

Publishing the models and their supporting datasets makes the resulting work easier to discover, evaluate and reuse in other cybersecurity tools and research workflows.

## Get involved

The project is open source. You can review the implementation, report issues and propose improvements in the [VulnTrain GitHub repository](https://github.com/vulnerability-lookup/VulnTrain). Consult the repository README for current setup, training and usage instructions.
