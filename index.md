<section id="about" class="section intro"><div class="container split"><div><p class="eyebrow">The toolkit</p><h2>Cyber threats evolve.<br>Defence must evolve faster.</h2></div><div class="prose"><p class="lead"><strong>AI-Powered Innovative Toolkit for Cybersecurity Hubs (AIPITCH)</strong> will provide a comprehensive set of practical, AI-powered tools for operational teams responsible for cyberdefence.</p><p>The project focuses especially on Security Operations Centres with national responsibility. Its integrated toolkit will also support teams protecting other constituencies—including enterprise SOCs—so they can strengthen key services, turn diverse signals into actionable intelligence and respond more effectively.</p><a class="source-link" href="https://science.nask.pl/en/research-areas/projects/12456" rel="noopener" target="_blank">View the project profile at NASK <span>↗</span></a></div></div></section>

<section id="objectives" class="section pale"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Our objectives</p><div class="section-heading"><h2>Research with a clear route<br>to real-world impact.</h2><p>From better intelligence to practical validation, the project connects advanced research with the day-to-day needs of national and enterprise cyberdefence teams.</p></div><div class="cards"><article><span class="card-no">01</span><div class="icon icon-eye">◉</div><h3>Detect earlier</h3><p>Apply AI methods to recognise evolving cyber threats and patterns across complex information sources.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">02</span><div class="icon">⌁</div><h3>Connect intelligence</h3><p>Transform fragmented technical evidence into structured, useful knowledge for analysts and response teams.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">03</span><div class="icon">◇</div><h3>Strengthen operations</h3><p>Support informed decisions and more effective delivery of essential cyberdefence services.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">04</span><div class="icon">◎</div><h3>Build trust</h3><p>Keep transparency, human oversight and responsible European AI principles embedded throughout the work.</p></article></div></div></section>
