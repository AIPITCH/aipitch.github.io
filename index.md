<section id="about" class="section intro"><div class="container split"><div><p class="eyebrow">The challenge</p><h2>Phishing evolves.<br>Defence must evolve faster.</h2></div><div class="prose"><p class="lead">Social engineering and phishing remain a persistent entry point for cyber attacks. Rapidly changing campaigns, growing volumes of data and increasingly convincing content demand a new generation of defensive tools.</p><p>AI-PITCH investigates how artificial intelligence can help cybersecurity teams identify suspicious activity, turn diverse signals into actionable intelligence and respond sooner—while keeping people, transparency and responsible use at the centre.</p><a class="source-link" href="https://science.nask.pl/en/research-areas/projects/12456" rel="noopener" target="_blank">View the project profile at NASK <span>↗</span></a></div></div></section>

<section id="objectives" class="section pale"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Our objectives</p><div class="section-heading"><h2>Research with a clear route<br>to real-world impact.</h2><p>From better intelligence to practical validation, the project connects advanced research with the day-to-day needs of cyber defenders.</p></div><div class="cards"><article><span class="card-no">01</span><div class="icon icon-eye">◉</div><h3>Detect earlier</h3><p>Explore AI methods that can recognise evolving phishing indicators and patterns across complex information sources.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">02</span><div class="icon">⌁</div><h3>Connect intelligence</h3><p>Transform fragmented technical evidence into structured, useful knowledge for analysts and response teams.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">03</span><div class="icon">◇</div><h3>Strengthen response</h3><p>Support faster, informed decisions and help organisations improve resilience to phishing-led attacks.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">04</span><div class="icon">◎</div><h3>Build trust</h3><p>Keep transparency, human oversight and responsible European AI principles embedded throughout the work.</p></article></div></div></section>
