<section id="about" class="section intro"><div class="container split"><div><p class="eyebrow">The toolkit</p><h2>Phishing evolves.<br>Defence must evolve faster.</h2></div><div class="prose"><p class="lead"><strong>AI-Powered Innovative Toolkit for Cybersecurity Hubs (AIPITCH)</strong> equips cyber defenders with a new generation of practical, AI-supported capabilities.</p><p>The project investigates how artificial intelligence can help cybersecurity teams identify suspicious activity, turn diverse signals into actionable intelligence and respond sooner—while keeping people, transparency and responsible use at the centre.</p><a class="source-link" href="https://science.nask.pl/en/research-areas/projects/12456" rel="noopener" target="_blank">View the project profile at NASK <span>↗</span></a></div></div></section>

<section id="objectives" class="section pale"><div class="container"><p class="eyebrow">Our objectives</p><div class="section-heading"><h2>Research with a clear route<br>to real-world impact.</h2><p>From better intelligence to practical validation, the project connects advanced research with the day-to-day needs of cyber defenders.</p></div><div class="cards"><article><span class="card-no">01</span><div class="icon icon-eye">◉</div><h3>Detect earlier</h3><p>Explore AI methods that can recognise evolving phishing indicators and patterns across complex information sources.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">02</span><div class="icon">⌁</div><h3>Connect intelligence</h3><p>Transform fragmented technical evidence into structured, useful knowledge for analysts and response teams.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">03</span><div class="icon">◇</div><h3>Strengthen response</h3><p>Support faster, informed decisions and help organisations improve resilience to phishing-led attacks.</p></article><article><span class="card-no">04</span><div class="icon">◎</div><h3>Build trust</h3><p>Keep transparency, human oversight and responsible European AI principles embedded throughout the work.</p></article></div></div></section>

<section id="work" class="section work"><div class="container work-grid"><div class="work-panel"><div class="mesh"></div><p>From signal</p><strong>to an integrated<br>toolkit</strong><div class="pipeline"><span>DATA</span><i></i><span>AI</span><i></i><span>ACTION</span></div></div><div><p class="eyebrow">The AIPITCH toolkit</p><h2>Designed around collaboration.</h2><div class="steps"><div><b>01</b><h3>Research</h3><p>Develop and assess AI techniques for analysing phishing-related information.</p></div><div><b>02</b><h3>Integrate</h3><p>Bring complementary expertise, data sources and tools into one coherent toolkit.</p></div><div><b>03</b><h3>Validate</h3><p>Evaluate toolkit capabilities against practical cybersecurity needs and operational realities.</p></div><div><b>04</b><h3>Share</h3><p>Make knowledge and results available to strengthen cybersecurity hubs across Europe.</p></div></div></div></div></section>

<section id="consortium" class="section consortium"><div class="container"><div class="section-heading"><div><p class="eyebrow">The consortium</p><h2>European expertise,<br>one shared ambition.</h2></div><p>NASK PIB is the leader of the consortium, and it is made up of the organizations:</p></div><ul class="partner-list"><li><strong>CIRCL/ LHC</strong><span>Computer Incident Response Center Luxembourg, Luxembourg</span></li><li><strong>Shadowserver Foundation</strong><span>Stichting The Shadowserver Foundation Europe, Netherlands</span></li><li><strong>NCBJ</strong><span>National Center for Nuclear Research, Poland</span></li><li><strong>ABI LAB</strong><span>Centre of Research and Innovation for Banks, Italy</span></li></ul><p class="consortium-budget">The project’s budget is almost <strong>€ 8 million</strong>, of which NASK PIB will account for less than <strong>€ 3 million</strong>.</p></div></section>
