We are pleased to announce the start of **AIPITCH — the AI-Powered Innovative Toolkit for Cybersecurity Hubs**.

Beginning in January 2025, this three-year European initiative brings together expertise from across the continent to develop a comprehensive suite of practical, AI-powered tools for operational cyberdefence teams. The project will focus particularly on Security Operations Centres with national responsibility, while also supporting enterprise SOCs and other teams that protect essential services.

Over the course of the project, the consortium will research, integrate and validate capabilities that help analysts recognise evolving threats, connect fragmented intelligence and make informed operational decisions. Transparency, human oversight and responsible European AI principles will remain central to this work.

We look forward to sharing our progress, research and project milestones here.
